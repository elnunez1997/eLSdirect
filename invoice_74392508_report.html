2 Allowance Computation Methodology
CDF_ (Off-Invoice / Customer Deal Fund) — per case:
Computed Off-Invoice per case = List Price − Order Price. This per-case amount is what the customer's deal authorizes. Multiplying by Qty Shipped gives the total off-invoice credit on the line. The system CDF_ field represents the deal-authorized per-case discount; the remaining delta between (List − Order) and CDF_ per case is absorbed by LDI_ (Logistic/Distribution Index allowance). In every valid row: CDF_/cs + LDI_/cs = (List − Order)/cs. This checks out on all 19 billable rows (rows 1–20, excluding 16b duplicate).
